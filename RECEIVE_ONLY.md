# XerMedia receive-only mode

XerMedia is configured as a receive-only WhatsApp inbox for XerService.

The active product surface is intentionally limited to Dashboard, Inbox, Contacts, Recent Activity, and Settings. Settings retains Inbox, Fields & tags, Team members, and API keys.

Outbound replies, reactions, broadcasts, templates, automations, flows, AI replies, pipelines, and deals are not part of the XerMedia product.

Incoming message records and mirrored inbound media use a three-day retention policy.
